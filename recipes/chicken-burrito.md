## Safety baseline

Cook poultry to 165°F (74°C). Refrigerate perishable food promptly in shallow containers, within two hours (one hour above 90°F); refrigerate at 40°F or below. Reheat leftovers to 165°F. For rice, cool quickly and refrigerate or freeze promptly; the UK Food Standards Agency recommends refrigerated rice be eaten within 24 hours. Freeze individual portions intended for later.

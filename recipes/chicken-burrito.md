# Easy Amazon chicken burrito

**Status:** First test in progress, November 15, 2026. Portions and seasoning are starting suggestions, not yet taste-tested.

## Goal

Source all ingredients from Amazon for easy reordering. Develop a repeatable fresh daily assembly system after the test, keeping warm components separate and adding cold toppings at assembly.

## One test burrito

Start with one tortilla, 3–4 oz chicken, 1/3 cup heated rice, 1/4 cup drained beans, 1/4 cup cooked peppers and onions, 2 tbsp cheese, 1–2 tbsp drained pico, 1–2 tbsp guacamole, and 1 tbsp sour cream. Adjust to fit tortilla and preference. Optional cumin, chili powder, and garlic powder; taste before adding salt because chicken and tortilla are already seasoned.

1. Cook frozen peppers and onions according to package until hot throughout and desired tenderness. Set aside.
2. Heat the fully cooked chicken from frozen according to package instructions. Follow package directions; heat thoroughly to 165°F. Chop and taste; optionally add small pinches of spices.
3. Heat rice pouch according to package. Measure 1/3 cup; promptly chill or freeze remaining rice in shallow portions. Do not leave at room temperature.
4. Drain and rinse beans; heat 1/4 cup with a splash of water. Refrigerate remaining beans in a covered container.
5. Warm tortilla in a dry skillet until pliable. Layer rice, beans, chicken, vegetables, cheese, drained pico, guacamole and sour cream in a compact strip. Fold sides in, tuck and roll; optionally toast seam-side down.
6. Record actual amounts and taste before scaling. Questions: flavor, salt, heat, moisture, filling balance, and ease of rolling.

Refrigerate leftovers promptly in shallow containers within two hours (one hour above 90°F); keep refrigerator at 40°F or below. Reheat leftovers to 165°F. Cool rice quickly, ideally within an hour; for a conservative approach use refrigerated rice within 24 hours or freeze portions promptly. Follow package storage instructions for opened products.
